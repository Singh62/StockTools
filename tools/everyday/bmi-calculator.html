BMI Calculator

Enter your height and weight to see your BMI and WHO category. BMI is a simple screening number, not a diagnosis.

How to use the BMI Calculator

  1. Choose metric or imperial units.
  2. Enter your height and weight.
  3. Press Calculate to see BMI, category and the healthy weight range for your height.

Formula and how it works

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

WHO adult categories: below 18.5 underweight; 18.5–24.9 normal; 25–29.9 overweight; 30 and above obese. Some health bodies use lower cut-offs for South Asian populations. BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat and is not suitable for children, pregnancy or athletes. This is general information, not medical advice: speak to a doctor about your health.

Example

Height 170 cm and weight 70 kg give a BMI of 24.2, in the normal range. The healthy range at that height is about 53.5–72.0 kg.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate?

It is a rough screening tool. It ignores muscle mass, age, sex and body-fat distribution.

Does the category differ in India?

Some Indian and Asian guidelines use lower thresholds (for example overweight from 23). Ask your doctor which applies to you.

Is my data stored?

No. Nothing is stored or sent.
