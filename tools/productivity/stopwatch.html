LapSplitTotal

Press Start to begin timing. Use Lap to record split times, Stop to pause and Reset to clear.

How to use the Stopwatch

  1. Press Start to begin.
  2. Press Lap at any time to record a split.
  3. Press Stop to pause; Resume continues from where it stopped.
  4. Reset clears the time and laps; Copy times copies them as text.

Formula and how it works

Time is measured with the browser's high-resolution clock (performance.now), so it stays accurate even if the display updates slowly. The display refreshes about 30 times a second and the timer is cleared when you leave the page. Background tabs may update the display less often, but elapsed time remains correct.

Example

Start, press Lap at 00:10.50 and again at 00:25.00: the laps show splits of 00:10.50 and 00:14.50 with totals 00:10.50 and 00:25.00.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is it?

Accurate to a few milliseconds in typical use; browsers deliberately limit clock precision for privacy.

Does it keep running if I switch tabs?

Yes, elapsed time is calculated from the clock, not by counting ticks.

Is anything saved?

No. Times exist only on this page.
