Date Difference Calculator

Pick a start and end date to see the difference as years, months and days, as total days and weeks, and as weekdays (Monday to Friday).

How to use the Date Difference Calculator

  1. Choose the start and end dates (order does not matter).
  2. Tick “Include the end date” if both days should count, e.g. for a leave or booking.
  3. Press Calculate.

Formula and how it works

Months are whole calendar months from the start date (using the last day of the month if the start day does not exist, e.g. 31 Jan + 1 month = 28/29 Feb); the leftover is days. Total days is the exact calendar-day difference, unaffected by time zones. Weekdays count Monday–Friday only; public holidays are not excluded. Including the end date adds one day.

Example

From 1 January 2024 to 1 March 2024: 2 months, 0 days, 60 total days (2024 is a leap year).

Frequently asked questions

Does the count include the end date?

Not by default. Tick the box to include it.

Are holidays excluded from weekdays?

No, only Saturdays and Sundays are excluded.

Can the end date be earlier?

Yes, the dates are swapped automatically and you are told.
