Age Calculator

Defaults to today

Enter a date of birth to see the exact age in years, months and days. You can also calculate age on any other date, for example for a form or eligibility cut-off.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Pick your date of birth.
  2. Leave “Age at” as today, or choose another date to find the age on that day.
  3. Press Calculate to see your age and next birthday.
  4. Use Copy result to share the summary.

Formula and how it works

The age is the largest number of whole years and months that can be added to the date of birth without passing the chosen date; the leftover are the days. If the birth day does not exist in a month (for example 31st in February, or 29 February in a non-leap year), the last day of that month is used. For a 29 February birthday, the next birthday in non-leap years is shown as 28 February.

Total days is the exact count of calendar days between the two dates. Dates are handled as plain calendar dates, so time zones and daylight-saving changes do not affect the result.

Example

Born on 15 January 2000, the age on 20 March 2024 is 24 years, 2 months, 5 days.

Frequently asked questions

Does it count the day of birth?

Age counts completed time since birth, so on the date of birth the age is 0 days and the next day is 1 day.

How is a 29 February birthday handled?

Month arithmetic uses the last day of the month when the date does not exist, so in non-leap years the birthday is treated as 28 February.

Can I find the age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the “Age at” date. The date of birth must not be after it.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. It is processed in your browser only and is not stored or sent to analytics.
