Unix Timestamp Converter

Timestamp → date

Date → timestamp

Paste a Unix timestamp to see it as a readable date in UTC, India time and your own time zone, or pick a date and time to get its timestamp.

How to use the Timestamp Converter

  1. To convert a timestamp, paste it and press Convert. Values of 11 digits or more are treated as milliseconds, otherwise seconds.
  2. Press “Use current time” to fill in the current timestamp.
  3. To convert the other way, choose a date, time and zone and press Get timestamp.
  4. Use Copy results to copy everything shown.

Formula and how it works

A Unix timestamp counts seconds since 1 January 1970 00:00:00 UTC (the epoch), ignoring leap seconds. Milliseconds timestamps (as used by JavaScript) are 1000 times larger. IST is UTC+5:30 with no daylight saving. The unit guess is a heuristic: a seconds value above 100 billion (the year 5138) would be misread as milliseconds.

Example

Timestamp 1700000000 is 2023-11-14 22:13:20 UTC, which is 15 Nov 2023, 3:43:20 am in India.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Year 2038 problem?

Systems storing seconds in a signed 32-bit integer overflow on 19 January 2038. This tool uses 64-bit numbers and is unaffected.

Seconds or milliseconds?

10 digits is usually seconds, 13 digits milliseconds. The tool picks one automatically and tells you which.

Does it include leap seconds?

No. Unix time ignores them.
