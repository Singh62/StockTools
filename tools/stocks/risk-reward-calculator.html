Risk Reward Ratio Calculator

For total ₹ amounts

For education and arithmetic only. Not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Trading and investing involve risk of loss; charges, taxes and market conditions will change real outcomes.

Before entering a trade, compare what you stand to lose with what you hope to gain. Enter entry, stop-loss and target prices to get the ratio.

How to use the Risk Reward Calculator

  1. Enter your entry price, stop-loss and target.
  2. Optionally enter the quantity to see total amounts.
  3. Press Calculate. For a buy: stop-loss < entry < target. For a short sale: target < entry < stop-loss.

Formula and how it works

Risk = |Entry − Stop|, Reward = |Target − Entry|
Ratio = Reward ÷ Risk
Break-even win rate = Risk ÷ (Risk + Reward)

The break-even win rate is the share of trades you must win for the strategy to break even before costs. With a 1:3 ratio you need to win 25% of trades. Charges lower real returns, and a good ratio does not make a trade likely to succeed.

Example

Entry ₹100, stop-loss ₹95, target ₹115: risk ₹5, reward ₹15, ratio 1 : 3, and you need to win only 25% of such trades to break even. With 50 shares that is ₹250 at risk for ₹750 of reward.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good risk-reward ratio?

It depends on your win rate. Higher ratios allow lower win rates, but they come with targets that are hit less often.

Why does it say my prices are invalid?

For a buy the stop must be below entry and the target above; for a short sale it is the reverse.

Does it include brokerage?

No. Use the Profit & Loss calculator for net figures.
