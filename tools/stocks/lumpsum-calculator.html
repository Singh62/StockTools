Lumpsum Calculator

An assumption, not a guarantee
Decimals allowed

For education and arithmetic only. Not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Trading and investing involve risk of loss; charges, taxes and market conditions will change real outcomes.

Enter a one-time investment amount, the yearly return you assume and the number of years to see an estimate of its future value.

How to use the Lumpsum Calculator

  1. Enter the amount you will invest once.
  2. Enter the yearly return you want to assume.
  3. Enter the number of years and press Calculate.

Formula and how it works

Future value = P × (1 + r)^t

P is the amount, r the yearly return as a decimal and t the years. It assumes a constant return compounded yearly with no withdrawals, taxes, fees or inflation adjustment. Real market returns vary every year.

Example

₹1,00,000 at an assumed 12% a year for 10 years grows to about ₹3,10,585, a gain of ₹2,10,585.

Frequently asked questions

What return should I assume?

Nobody can know future returns; try a few conservative values to see the range.

Lumpsum or SIP?

A lumpsum invests everything at once; a SIP spreads it monthly. Use the SIP Calculator for the latter.

Is the result after tax?

No. It is before taxes, fees and inflation.
