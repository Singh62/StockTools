SWP Calculator

An assumption
Up to 60

For education and arithmetic only. Not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Trading and investing involve risk of loss; charges, taxes and market conditions will change real outcomes.

A systematic withdrawal plan takes a fixed amount out of an investment every month while the rest keeps growing. Enter your corpus, monthly withdrawal, assumed return and period.

How to use the SWP Calculator

  1. Enter your starting corpus and the amount you want each month.
  2. Enter the yearly return you assume and the period.
  3. Press Calculate to see the balance left or how long the corpus lasts.

Formula and how it works

Each month the withdrawal is taken first, then the remaining balance grows by r ÷ 12 (r = yearly return). If the balance cannot cover a withdrawal, the plan ends. Constant returns are assumed; real returns fluctuate, so a poor start can drain a corpus faster than shown. Taxes, exit loads and inflation are not included.

Example

₹10,00,000 with ₹8,000 withdrawn monthly at an assumed 8% for 10 years leaves about ₹7,46,315 after withdrawing ₹9,60,000 in total.

Frequently asked questions

What is a safe withdrawal amount?

There is no guaranteed safe rate; lower withdrawals relative to the corpus last longer.

Why does the corpus run out?

If withdrawals exceed growth, the balance shrinks and eventually cannot cover a withdrawal.

Is SWP income taxable?

Gains are taxable as capital gains; the rules vary and are not modelled here.
