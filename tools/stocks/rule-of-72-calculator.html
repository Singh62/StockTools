Rule of 72 Calculator

The Rule of 72 is a quick mental shortcut: divide 72 by the yearly return to estimate the years needed to double your money. Enter a return, or enter years to find the return required.

How to use the Rule of 72 Calculator

  1. To find the doubling time, enter the yearly return.
  2. To find the return needed, leave the return empty and enter the years.
  3. Press Calculate to see the rule's estimate next to the exact value.

Formula and how it works

Years ≈ 72 ÷ rate% Exact: ln 2 ÷ ln(1 + rate/100)

The rule is most accurate for returns of about 6–10%. It assumes yearly compounding with no withdrawals, taxes or fees.

Example

At 8% a year, money doubles in about 9 years (exact 9.01). To double in 6 years you need roughly 12% a year (exact 12.25%).

Frequently asked questions

Why 72?

It is close to 100 × ln 2 (69.3) and divides evenly by many common rates, which makes mental maths easy.

Is it exact?

No, it is an approximation; the exact value is shown next to it.

Does it guarantee my money will double?

No. It only does arithmetic on the rate you assume.
