Margin and Leverage Calculator

From your broker, e.g. 20 for 5x
Positive or negative

For education and arithmetic only. Not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Trading and investing involve risk of loss; charges, taxes and market conditions will change real outcomes.

Margin lets you control a larger position with less cash. Enter the price, quantity and your broker's margin percentage to see exposure, leverage and what a price move does to your capital.

How to use the Margin & Leverage Calculator

  1. Enter the price and quantity.
  2. Enter the margin percentage your broker requires.
  3. Enter the price move you want to test (use a negative number for a fall).
  4. Press Calculate.

Formula and how it works

Exposure = Price × Qty
Margin = Exposure × Margin%
Leverage = 100 ÷ Margin%
P&L = Exposure × Move%, Return on margin = P&L ÷ Margin

Leverage multiplies gains and losses. A price fall equal to the margin percentage wipes out the margin entirely, and brokers may close positions earlier. Margin rules differ by product and change. Interest, brokerage and charges are not included.

Example

100 shares at ₹500 with 20% margin: exposure ₹50,000, margin ₹10,000, leverage 5x. A 2% rise gives ₹1,000 (10% on margin); a 20% fall would wipe out the margin.

Frequently asked questions

What is leverage in trading?

Using borrowed or margined money to hold a bigger position than your cash alone allows.

Is leveraged trading risky?

Yes. Losses are magnified and can exceed what you expect, especially with gaps. Understand the risks before trading.

Where do I find my margin percentage?

In your broker's margin calculator or product details for that stock and product type.
