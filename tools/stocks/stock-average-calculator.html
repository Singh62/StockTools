Stock Average Calculator

Plan: reach a target average (optional)
Between your average and the current price

For education and arithmetic only. Not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Trading and investing involve risk of loss; charges, taxes and market conditions will change real outcomes.

Bought the same stock more than once? Enter the units and price of each purchase to get your average buy price. Optionally enter the current price and a target average to see how many more shares you would need.

How to use the Stock Average Calculator

  1. Enter units and price per share for each purchase. Two rows are shown; use “+ Add purchase” for more (up to 20).
  2. Leave a row empty if you do not need it.
  3. To plan averaging, open the optional section and enter today's price and the average you want.
  4. Press Calculate. Results update as you edit.

Formula and how it works

Average price = Σ(units × price) ÷ Σ(units)

Shares to buy for a target: n = U × (A − T) ÷ (T − P), where U is your total units, A your current average, T the target average and P the current price. The target must lie between A and P, otherwise no number of shares can reach it. The result is rounded up to whole shares.

Brokerage, taxes and other charges are not included. Fractional units are allowed for fractional-share accounts.

Example

100 shares at ₹50 and 50 shares at ₹40 cost ₹7,000 for 150 shares: an average of ₹46.67. If the price is now ₹30 and you want an average of ₹40, you would need to buy 100 more shares (₹3,000), giving exactly ₹40.

Frequently asked questions

What is averaging down?

Buying more of a stock after its price has fallen so that your average cost drops. It lowers your break-even price but also increases the money at risk in one stock.

Is averaging down a good idea?

Not automatically. It is only sensible if you still believe in the company at the lower price. Adding to a falling stock can magnify losses.

Does the average include brokerage?

No. Add charges to the price of each purchase if you want a cost-inclusive average.

Is my data saved?

No. Everything stays in your browser.
