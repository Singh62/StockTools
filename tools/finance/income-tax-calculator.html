Income Tax Calculator (FY 2025-26)

Salary or business income before deductions
80C, 80D, home-loan interest etc. (optional)

Results are illustrative estimates based on the assumptions stated on this page. They are not financial, tax or legal advice. Actual figures from banks, fund houses or the tax authorities can differ.

Enter your annual income and, for the old regime, your total deductions. You will see the tax under both regimes side by side and which is lower. Supported year: FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), resident individuals below 60.

How to use the Income Tax Calculator

  1. Enter your total annual income.
  2. Enter the total of old-regime deductions you would claim (leave blank for none). The new regime ignores most deductions.
  3. Keep “Salaried” ticked to apply the standard deduction (₹75,000 new, ₹50,000 old).
  4. Press Calculate and compare the two results.

Formula and how it works

New regime (FY 2025-26): 0–4L nil; 4–8L 5%; 8–12L 10%; 12–16L 15%; 16–20L 20%; 20–24L 25%; above 24L 30%. Section 87A rebate up to ₹60,000 when taxable income is up to ₹12 lakh, with marginal relief just above it.

Old regime: 0–2.5L nil; 2.5–5L 5%; 5–10L 20%; above 10L 30%. Rebate up to ₹12,500 when taxable income is up to ₹5 lakh.

Health and education cess of 4% is added to both. Not included: surcharge (so taxable income above ₹50 lakh is rejected), senior-citizen slabs, capital-gains special rates, TDS credits and employer-specific perks. Tax rules change every Budget: verify against the Income Tax Department before filing.

Example

Salaried income ₹15,00,000 with ₹1,50,000 deductions: new regime taxable income is ₹14,25,000 and tax is ₹97,500 (including cess); old regime taxable income is ₹13,00,000 and tax is ₹2,10,600. The new regime is lower here.

Frequently asked questions

Which regime is better for me?

It depends on your deductions. The calculator shows both so you can compare; the old regime tends to win only when deductions are large.

Does it work for senior citizens?

No. It covers resident individuals below 60 only; senior and super-senior slabs differ.

Why is income above ₹50 lakh rejected?

Surcharge and its marginal relief apply there and are not modelled, so any figure would be misleading.

Is this tax advice?

No. It is an illustrative estimate. Confirm with the official calculator or a tax professional before filing.
