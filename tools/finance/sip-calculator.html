SIP Calculator

A SIP (systematic investment plan) invests a fixed amount every month. Enter the monthly amount, the annual return you assume and the number of years to see an estimate of the final corpus.

How to use the SIP Calculator

  1. Enter how much you plan to invest each month.
  2. Enter the annual return you want to assume. Returns of market-linked products are not guaranteed.
  3. Enter the investment period in years.
  4. Press Calculate to see invested amount, estimated returns and maturity value.

Formula and how it works

FV = P × [((1 + i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i)

P is the monthly instalment, i is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months. The extra (1 + i) assumes each instalment is invested at the start of the month. If the rate is 0%, FV = P × n.

Assumptions: a constant return every month, no exit load, expense ratio, taxes or inflation adjustment. Real market returns vary from year to year.

Example

Investing ₹5,000 a month for 10 years at an assumed 12% per year gives an invested amount of ₹6,00,000 and an estimated maturity value of about ₹11,61,695, i.e. estimated returns of ₹5,61,695.

Frequently asked questions

What return should I enter?

Nobody can know future returns. Use a conservative figure and try a few values to see the range of outcomes. Past performance of any fund does not guarantee future results.

Does this include taxes or inflation?

No. The estimate is before taxes, fund charges and inflation, so the real purchasing power of the result will be lower.

Why is my fund's actual value different?

Actual SIP returns depend on the NAV on each instalment date, so they fluctuate. This tool assumes a smooth, constant monthly rate.

Can I use it for a recurring deposit?

It is built for market-linked SIPs, but the same maths gives an approximation for any monthly saving at a fixed rate. Banks compound RDs quarterly, so their maturity amount will differ a little.
