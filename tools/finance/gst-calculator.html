GST Calculator

Results are illustrative estimates based on the assumptions stated on this page. They are not financial, tax or legal advice. Actual figures from banks, fund houses or the tax authorities can differ.

Use this tool to calculate GST on a price (GST-exclusive) or to find the original price and tax from an amount that already includes GST.

How to use the GST Calculator

  1. Enter the amount in rupees.
  2. Choose “Add GST to amount” if the amount is the price before tax, or “Remove GST from amount” if it already includes GST.
  3. Pick the GST rate that applies to your goods or service, or enter a custom rate.
  4. Press Calculate to see the net amount, total GST and the CGST + SGST (same state) or IGST (inter-state) split.

Formula and how it works

Add GST: GST = Amount × Rate ÷ 100, Total = Amount + GST
Remove GST: Net = Amount × 100 ÷ (100 + Rate), GST = Amount − Net

For supplies within one state, GST is split equally into CGST and SGST. For supplies between states, the full amount is IGST.

Check your rate: GST rates and slabs are revised by the GST Council from time to time and differ by product or service. The rate list here is a convenience; confirm the correct rate for your item from the official GST notifications or your tax advisor. Invoice-level rounding rules can make final invoices differ by a few paise.

Example

₹1,000 with 18% GST added: GST is ₹180 (CGST ₹90 + SGST ₹90) and the total is ₹1,180. Going the other way, ₹1,180 that includes 18% GST has a net price of ₹1,000.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST goes to the central government and SGST to the state government when buyer and seller are in the same state. IGST is charged on inter-state supplies.

How do I calculate GST from an inclusive price?

Divide the inclusive amount by (1 + rate ÷ 100) to get the net price, then subtract it from the total to get the GST. This tool does it for you.

Which GST rate applies to my product?

It depends on the HSN/SAC classification and the notifications in force at the time of supply. Check the official GST rate finder or ask your accountant.

Can I use a rate that is not in the list?

Yes. Choose “Custom…” and enter any rate between 0 and 100.
