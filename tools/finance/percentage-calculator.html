Percentage Calculator

Pick the question you want answered, enter two numbers and get the answer with the formula shown. Handy for marks, discounts, price changes and growth rates.

How to use the Percentage Calculator

  1. Choose the type of calculation from the drop-down.
  2. Enter the two numbers. The labels change to match your choice.
  3. Press Calculate. The answer appears with the formula used.
  4. Edit a number to update the answer instantly.

Formula and how it works

X% of Y = X × Y ÷ 100
X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100
Change % = (New − Old) ÷ |Old| × 100
Y is X% of ? = Y ÷ X × 100

Percentage change is undefined when the old value is 0. When the old value is negative the absolute value is used so that an increase always shows as positive.

Example

15% of 200 is 30. 25 is 12.5% of 200. A price rising from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase. 50 is 20% of 250.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100. A positive result is an increase, a negative one a decrease.

Why can't I use 0 as the starting value?

Dividing by zero is undefined, so percentage change from 0 cannot be calculated.

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

If a rate rises from 10% to 12%, it rises by 2 percentage points, which is a 20% relative increase.

How precise are the answers?

Results are shown with up to 6 decimal places, with floating-point noise removed.
