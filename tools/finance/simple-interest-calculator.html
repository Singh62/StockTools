Simple Interest Calculator

Decimals allowed, e.g. 1.5

Results are illustrative estimates based on the assumptions stated on this page. They are not financial, tax or legal advice. Actual figures from banks, fund houses or the tax authorities can differ.

Simple interest is charged only on the original principal. Enter the principal, annual rate and time in years to see the interest and total amount.

How to use the Simple Interest Calculator

  1. Enter the principal amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the time in years (use decimals for months, e.g. 0.5).
  4. Press Calculate.

Formula and how it works

SI = P × R × T ÷ 100, Amount = P + SI

P is the principal, R the annual rate in percent and T the time in years. Interest does not earn interest. For interest on interest see the Compound Interest Calculator.

Example

₹1,00,000 at 8% for 3 years earns ₹24,000 interest, so the total amount is ₹1,24,000.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest is computed on the principal only; compound interest is computed on principal plus accumulated interest.

How do I enter months?

Divide by 12: 6 months is 0.5 years, 18 months is 1.5 years.

Where is simple interest used?

Some short-term loans, certain bonds and many textbook problems. Most bank deposits and loans compound.
