Discount Calculator

Applied after the first

Enter the original price and the discount percentage to get the sale price and how much you save. Add a second discount to see successive offers, such as 20% off plus an extra 10%.

How to use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Optionally enter a second discount that applies to the already-reduced price.
  4. Press Calculate to see the final price and savings.

Formula and how it works

Final = Price × (1 − d₁/100) × (1 − d₂/100)
Effective discount = (Price − Final) ÷ Price × 100

Two successive discounts are not added together: 20% then 10% is a 28% total discount, not 30%.

Example

A ₹2,000 item at 20% off plus an extra 10% costs ₹1,440. You save ₹560, an effective discount of 28%.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't 20% + 10% equal to 30%?

The second discount applies to the reduced price, so it removes less than 10% of the original.

Does this include GST?

No. If the price excludes GST, add it with the GST Calculator.

Can I find the original price from the sale price?

Divide the sale price by (1 − discount/100). The Percentage Calculator's reverse mode can help.
